### Notes:
The elusive and all, encompassing mist is perhaps spiritkind's worst enemy, and greatest asset at the same time. It is also our driver of isolation, a conflicting thought I muse over during hours of pondering. The people in [[Arlemany]] seem to place too much fear into mentions of the mist, blinding them to how powerful and influential [[Mist Science]] can be. I once had a patient discussion with some of the peasants about what the mist meant to our world and livelihood, and it led me to the conclusion I have today, though to the uneducated, the powers of the mist is indeed a thing of intimidation.
