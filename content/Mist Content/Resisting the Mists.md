Throughout the years, those who seek to actively fight the effects of [[The Mists|the mists]] have discovered forces that can repel mist. As a result, these resources are often used in mist protective devices, as well as stations that prevents the mist from corrupting a city or region. Oddly enough, mist-repelling metals are often made from metals that has been infused with the mist. Although the exact reason behind this is not yet known, it is theorized that materials supercharged with the mist gives off an invisible repelling force to other forms mist energies, including the haze that covers the world. As a result, most of these Mist-Repelling Resources comes from mist-infused metals, albeit a stabilized and/or tempered version of one.

### Mist-Repelling Resources

##### Mistdome
^a9a61a
A mistdome is a large city-wide net of mist-repelling metal that forms safe spaces where cities and towns can thrive without the effect of [[The Mists|the mists]]. Considered the most powerful currently-known Mist-repelling structure, Mistdomes are employed only by large settlements or nation remnants, who has enough stored wealth to afford such a large infrastructure project.
