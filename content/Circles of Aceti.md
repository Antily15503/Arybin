The Circles of Aceti, or the Acetic Circle (AKA Acetic Path, Main Acetician Following, or Malobar Faith) is the standard main branch of the Aceti Faiths. It is the prevalent faith in the [[Lorint]] continent, and worships the presence of the Goddess **Acetica**, and her holy council of "worldly governors" that symbolizes the many forces and influences of life and death, good and evil, and wealth and suffering.

### Theology
The Main branch of Aceti, also known as the Circles of Aceti, sees the world as a makeup of different forces of light and dark that serves to counterbalance each other. the **circles** in the name of the faith symbolizes this counterbalancing act, since each light and dark force forms a circle of completion, a circle that perpetuates balance in the world. The supreme goddess **Acetica** is a holy patronage that seeks to uphold this balance of power, of maintaining and balancing the idea of "circles" in the world, a task she takes with the utmost sincerity and respect. Her court, known as the "court of worldly deities" is her way of balancing the world, by striking down forces that overstep and threaten to disrupt the delicate balance of the world, while maintaining harmony.

As earthly followers in the Circles of Aceti, the creatures of Lorint are also tasked to protect the balance of life by living a life of respect for both the light and dark forces. Light sources provide energy and determination to carry on, while the dark sources provide wisdom, insight, and the freedom to choose one's own path. To live in the balance of both sources is pious in the eyes of **Acetia**, while any action that seeks to disrupt this balance for oneself and for others around them is a sign of danger.

To help people to live in balance, the goddess **Acetica** provides several theological commandments / decrees to live by. Although they began as a simple account of her words, passed down from ancient history, they have since been seen as a "holy code" to live in accordance by.
