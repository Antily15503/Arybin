A mist creature is a creature that has been enchanted by the mist. Corrupted, unfiltered arcane energy is forced into such creature, to the point that the creature is no longer alive, but merely powered by a blind form of magic. Most of the Mist Creatures are deadly, though how deadly they are depends on how concentrated of a Mist fills their presence

##### [[Low Threat Mistlings]]
A low threat mistling is a mistling that is either weak and thus can be defeated easily (unless in a swarm), or a mistling that does not display aggressive tendencies unless provoked. Some Low Threat Mistlings have been reported to have blended with the human/elvic population, though their stunning concealment makes them foes to be wary of.
