The Mists (collective) references a hazy substance that are the boundary between different cities and continents in the [[Kalaile|Sea of Continents]]. They are an immensely powerful and deadly force, as spending too much time in the mists without protection leads to a quick and painful death. 

Some speculates that the mists are a deadly curse left by [[The Mist King]], though others believe they were here long before, and only amplified by the actions of [[The Mist King]]. Nevertheless, a deadly supernatural force is what most sees the Mist as, and due to it's unpredictable and ominous nature, defense against the Mist is humanity's last chance.

In reality, the Mist is but a pure arcane force, materialized into a visible form due to the potency of magic held within. Some cities have learned to use The Mists as a source of power and alchemy fuel, though its unstable nature makes others wary of using The Mist, despite it's abundance.

The Mist is also capable of corrupting lower-sentient animals and creatures. For more details, see [[Mist Creatures]]
