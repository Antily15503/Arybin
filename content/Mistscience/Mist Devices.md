
**Mistbeacon**
A mistbeacon, or mistprobe (small mistbeacon), is a powerful signal amplifier that produces a form of wave that travels through the mist. Mistbeacons are usually used by cities to output a constant location.

They're usually used as location devices, allowing travelers unfortunate enough to have to travel into the mist to know exactly where they are in relation to nearby cities/towns/other mist protected pockets.
