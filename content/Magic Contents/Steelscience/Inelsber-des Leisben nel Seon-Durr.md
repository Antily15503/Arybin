The beauty of Steelscience lies in it's reliance of the self. The power of one's own will to be able to tap into the magic in their surroundings, the power of the inner mind in shaping the outside, the power of Steelscience is beautiful in its independence. Steelscience is deeply conservational in its resources, using simple common metals as channels for something more powerful and constructive, it does little to alter the nature of its conduits, a trait in which [[A Guide to Lightmancy|Lightmancy]] is inferior in. 

Steelscience is limited only by the strength of one's mind. After all, energy is everywhere, and metal is cheap. To be able to put these elements together in your head, and direct it to productive outcomes, is the real challenge of Steelscience. 

This literature is not an analysis of how this channel connects minds to metals, nor is it notes on the various unknowns surrounding Steelscience. Think of this as a guide.
