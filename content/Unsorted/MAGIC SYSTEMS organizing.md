 Notes: All magic systems are built from the idea of spectrum. The magic itself is an encompassment of a pure force that comes in different spectrums.
Ex: Lightmagic encompasses light, which is a pure force, though the colors represent the spectrums of light

Illusioles encompasses illusions, which are metaphysical forces that act on light. The spectrum of illusions encompasses all the different forces that can bend, alter, warp light, or even force light to quantum through different timelines. 

~~Wand magic encompasses conductance, a metallic property that is the passing of light magic through a medium. The spectrum encompasses how conductive a metal is, and how the light behaves with the metal within it.~~

Lastly, Mistbending encompasses the mist, a pure medium of energy that is deadly in it's chaos and uncertainty. Because it is a pure medium, it can be channeled to different "spectral forms", similar to light.

**Illusiole**
Illusioles are people with the power to cast illusions and portals to alternate worlds. By bending the power of the mist to create visual dissonances, one can "phase" out of the current world, and enter another world, either in the future, past, or other realms. These dissonances are called Illumens.

Although one Illusiole only has the power to cast illusions or disrupt sight around them, multiple Illusiole can pool their powers together to create a small, or even large Illumen.
