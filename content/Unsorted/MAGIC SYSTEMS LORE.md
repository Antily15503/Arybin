Lightmancy + Steelscience relies on the **Auralight**, a power formed by the convergence of Providence and Decay. Neither can control the power while the other is alive, and so Auralight remains (for now) a force of neutral power

The Auralight is actually the result of both Providence and Decay channeling their own powers to will it into existence. The nature of Providence perfectly cancelled out the destructive urge of Decay, and as a result the Auralight is a substance of pure power not controlled by either "deities"

The Gods:

The spirit of **Providence**
- Shattered by the spirit of Decay 
	- Balance - fragment of moderation
	- Revival - fragment of support
	- Empathy - fragment of spirit
	- Order - fragments of the stable
- the Theri Crystals is a manifestation of the powers of PROVIDENCE.

The spirit of **Decay**
- One world controlling spirit with different "personas", known as the spirits of Chaos:
	- Ruin - the destructive spirit
	- Undoing - the non-conforming spirit
	- Entropy - the chaotic spirit
	- Death - the conspiring spirit
- the Mists is a manifestation of the powers of DECAY.

The mists
- The physical manifestation Decay. However, the mists is merely pure power, and as a result can be contaminated by powers Decay may not want contaminating it.
	- White Mist - the mist of chaos \[Decay]
	- Gray Mist - the mist of flow \[Providence]
	- Black Mist - the mist of decay \[Decay]
	- Red Mist - the mist of blood \[Providence]
	- Yellow Mist - the mist of emotions \[Decay]
	- Blue Mist - the mist of thought \[Providence]
	- Violet Mist - the mist of condensed power of good and evil \[Conflicted]
