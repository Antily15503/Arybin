
The spirit of Providence
- Shattered by the spirit of Decay 
	- Balance - fragment of moderation
	- Revival - fragment of support
	- Empathy - fragment of spirit
	- Knowledge - fragments of the wisen

The spirit of Decay
- One world controlling spirit with different "personas", known as the spirits of Chaos:
	- Ruin - the destructive spirit
	- Undoing - the non-conforming spirit
	- Entropy - the chaotic spirit
	- Death - the conspiring spirit

The mists
- The physical manifestation of the imbalance of powers between Providence and Decay
	- White Mist - the mist of chaos
	- Gray Mist - the mist of flow
	- Black Mist - the mist of decay
	- Red Mist - the mist of blood
	- Yellow Mist - the mist of emotions
	- Blue Mist - the mist of thought
	- Violet Mist - the mist of condensed power of good and evil
