Ruled by **High King** Tenzihal (Accio Tenzihal), who owns the **High Theri Crystal** that keeps the entire Purelands pure and clean.
- Rules in **conjunction** with the **Nobles** of Arlemany and the Rural Regions.
- The High King is technically landless, as he only owns the Theri Crystal. However, in exchange for the protection and bountiful land the crystal provides, ALL OTHER nations within the protection of the Purelands are subservient under him. 
- It is said that he has the power to block where the Theri crystal can protect, although he has **never** used the power in front of mortals.

#### Nobles of Arlemany
- High and wealthy nobles that has solidified control and ownership over major industries within the High Pureland Regions.
- Those that work for them are partially owned by the nobles, though some chooses to pay their workers wages, while others do not but provides for them instead.
- Most nobles are famous for their ruthless capitalism, to the point where assassinations used to be common within the High Pureland Court.
- Although every other week, the nobles have a parade and a dinner / ball, where most of the nobles meet up to discuss business, intrigue, or simply dance and enjoy the luxury thrown by the host family.

**Lifestyle**
- Life of a noble in Arlemany is very indulging and grandiouse, however extremely stressful. Court intrigue is rampant, and the words of one nobleman must be scoured for hidden meanings, as lies and secret hints are almost as established of a language as the normal tongue.
