Ruled by **High King** Tenzihal (Accio Tenzihal), who owns the **High Theri Crystal** that keeps the entire Purelands pure and clean.
- Rules in **conjunction** with the **Nobles** of Arlemany and the Rural Regions.
- The High King is technically landless, as he only owns the Theri Crystal. However, in exchange for the protection and bountiful land the crystal provides, ALL OTHER nations within the protection of the Purelands are subservient under him. 
- It is said that he has the power to block where the Theri crystal can protect, although he has **never** used the power in front of mortals.
