Party members: 
Initially, the party members do not know each other. Each person, exactly one month after receiving the note of initiation, where their power's origins are revealed to be a secret god-metal that granted them immense and destructive powers when consumed, is invited to participate in the upcoming Gala by the Efreman Nobles. 

Each party member will masquerade as distant visiting nobles / emissaries of other high noble houses (from other parts of the Purelands), and as a result will have alias names.

Party members will keep within the inner shirt of their suits/gowns a paper symbol, of a C with a crossed x inside, a mark of the party member. This symbol must be emphasized that it can NOT be shown to anyone else, as they may risk their lives upon discovery.
