
### Days of the Week
A week is usually 6 days, and keeps track of the intensity of the mist. The mist, although it appears stationary and constant, comes in cycles of weak mist and strong mist. Weak mist days are usually days of optimal travel, while strong mist days means travel should be prohibited unless absolutely necessary.

**Mistcrest** (1)
Usually the day with the highest Mist activity. Mist travel should be prohibited unless absolutely necessary

**Fallsday** (2)
Mist activity is not as high as it is during Mistcrest, however traveling is not advised

**Moonsday** (3)
Mist activity is perhaps lowest. Traveling if needed is advised to be done on this day.

**Soulsday** (4)
Mist activity is also low, though there's a slight chance of [[Mist Attacks]]. Traveling if needed is also advised to be done on this day unless there is nearby [[Mist Attacks]] reported.

**Mistrise** (5)
Mist activity is a bit higher and steadily climbing. Traveling should only be done if you can reach other bubble within a day.

**Mistclimb** (6)
Mist activity shall reach Mistcrest levels later on this day. Do not travel unless absolute necessary.
