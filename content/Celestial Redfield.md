The Celestial Redfields, or Central Redfields, is the geographic central region of the Redfields continent. It is bordered by the [[Sea of Marbor]] to the East, the [[Dalmencoran Redlands]]  to the North, and the [[Ibenari Redlands]] and [[Sea of Adrathica]] to the West. 

The Celestial Redfields often pride itself for being some of the most economically productive regions in the world. Most of the Celestial Redfields sits in the **Great Red Plain**, a geographically flat region of the Redfields that is well irrigated with natural rivers, most of them flowing from the North, originating in the Dalmencoran region. This makes the Celestial Redfields one of the largest producers in luxury foods, such as spices, wine, fruits, herbs, and [[lightspice]], a group of plants that stimulates the bodies of people. 

**Society**
Most of the population in the Celestial Redfields work as laborers on farms, with most of them ruled over by a local Lord or Lady. Serfdom is very common through the Celestial Redfields, where laborers and farmers are paid a "generous" allowance in honor of the Lord Ruler, in return for their services in tending to their lands, and their harvest come harvest season. 

**History**
Most of the Celestial Redfields in history has been owned by a myriad of different duchies and local lords.
