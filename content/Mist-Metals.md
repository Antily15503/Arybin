**Vemistrine**
Vemistrine, a cloud white gemstone, is a gemstone that is formed when mist condenses on any other geode. Although a powerful channeler, it is known to corrupt existing gemstones, leeching them of their color and filling them with a cloud white.  

**Schissmistium**\
A very rare form of Mist-metal, a metal that is sought after by the richest of merchants and city officials. Schissmistium is an elemental mist-turned mineral that is the building block for most Mist-metal-based trinkets and gemstones.
