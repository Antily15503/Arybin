Alloy Science is the science that gives weapons and staffs that are imbuned with specific alloys of simple and rare metals the ability to perform magical actions.

All Magical Alloys consists of a blend of three basic metal components, which are blend in specific ratios to one another to form Magical Alloys. 
- An Anchor Metal
- A Resonator Metal
- A Trace Metal

#### **Anchor Metals**
Metals that anchor the magical component to the weapon itself. Often a sturdy hardy metal, anchor metals keeps the weapon bound to it's magical component and stabilizes any magical spells that are cast with it.

Basic Anchor Metals:
Iron
- Known for it's versatility and solidness
Copper
- Known for it's cheapness and ductility
Lead
- Known for it's heavy sturdiness and balance
Tungsten
- Known for it's powerful conductance of magic
Aluminum
- Known for it's light weight and quick channeling

Mixed Anchor Metals:

Bronze (Compound Anchor of Tin and Copper)
- Known for it's vibrancy and acute focus
Pewter (Compound Anchor of Tin and Lead)
- Known for it's cheapness and sturdiness
