The Central Province, also known as the Homelands, is a large (for this world's sake) area of land protected by the [[Crystal of Spiren]], a large and infinitely pure crystal of Mist-repelling aura that seeks to dispel the mist to only the borders of the province. 

Due to this crystal, the piece of land that is not affected by the crystal is one of the few places in this dark plane that has vegetation, life, and an abundance of animals. 

However, every day, the crystal becomes weaker, and adventurers must find a way to constantly fuel this crystal, or risk losing the city they have built

The Homeland also has several connections to [[Mistpaths]] to other nearby cities and towns. Each of these [[Mistpaths|mistpaths]] branch from what's called a Homeland Gate, which acts as an entranceway in and out of the Homeland region. Some notable [[Mistpaths|mistpaths]] include:

[[Southpass]] in Southpass Gate, which leads to [[Erfyrm]]

[[Northpass]] in Northpass Gate, which leads to [[Qirzen]]

[[Wishmen Pass]] in Ratanelle, which leads to [[Helsnet]]
