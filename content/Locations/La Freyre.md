#### The Pits of La Freyre
Locked to the party unless they complete the Freyre questline
- Find the Timekeeper Key and give it to the Head Guard who is stationed in front of the pits.
- Exchange the favor the Head Guard gives in return with the King of Freyreille, who will grant the party a pass to use the Pits of La Freyre as an independent merchant group.

The pits is engineered to contain many different layers. Each layer is filled with different kinds of [[Mistling]] or [[Mist-born]]s, however they all contain unmined [[Mist-Metals#^cea651|Mistite]], [[Mist-Metals#^291145|Schissmistium]], and other loots / treasures left by forgotten pitdivers.

*Layer 1: Virellio*

*Layer 2: Masellio*

*Layer 3: Falngacio*
